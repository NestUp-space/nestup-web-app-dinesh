# About Us

## From a dusty workshop to a revolution in modular interiors

Nine years ago, in my third year of college, I found myself helping my dad set up a CNC router in his humble woodworking shop. I had no idea that those late nights learning software and training his team would plant the first seeds of a much larger journey.

After graduating, I worked in the corporate world for a while. But the real calling came when I teamed up with a close friend to start an interior design firm. The creative satisfaction was immense, but so were the challenges—manual carpentry teams, unreliable timelines, and frustrating compromises on quality and cost. We knew there had to be a better way.

And that’s how ***Nestup*** was born.

Nestup is not just a modular factory.
It’s a system, a mindset, and a promise to fix what’s broken in the interiors industry.

With years of hands-on experience, countless mistakes (and lessons), and a deep understanding of what designers and homeowners truly need, we built Nestup from the ground up to be faster, smarter, and more reliable. We’ve automated the chaos—turning weeks of on-site carpentry into a streamlined, factory-led process that’s clean, predictable, and high-quality.

Our factory uses cutting-edge CNC tech, automated pressing, and precise edge banding to craft modular units that are built to last and made to fit. Every screw hole, hinge notch, and groove is calculated down to the millimeter. And the best part? Once materials are received, we commit to dispatch-ready modulars in just 14 working days.

## Why Nestup Exists

Because in a country where groceries arrive in 10 minutes and UPI payments are done in a blink—why does interior work still take 2-3 months?

We believe convenience is no longer a luxury. It’s a necessity.

**Our vision is simple:** Walk into a Nestup showroom, choose your designs, and have your woodwork installed within a day. We’re not there yet, but we’re getting close—fast.

We’re not just building wardrobes and kitchens.
We’re building a new standard for how interiors should be done in India.
