Hello, interior design professionals! Today, I want to share a game-changing lesson we've learned at Nestup Space that has revolutionized our modular fitout projects. By prioritizing one crucial change in our work process, we have significantly minimized rework and achieved precision in our designs. In this blog post, I will explore the power of starting with accurate site measurements and considering site realities during the design phase.

## The Challenge

In our last firm, we faced a common issue in our interior design projects. Despite detailed planning based on client-provided floor plans and working diagrams, we encountered discrepancies during execution.

Switchboard placements, cross walls, beams, and other site realities often didn't align with our initial designs, leading to rework and delays.

## Starting with Accurate Site Measurements

Recognizing the importance of precise information, we began by conducting thorough site measurements with mm-level accuracy. This hands-on approach allowed us to intimately understand the space and its potential constraints.

## Considering Site Realities

With accurate measurements, we incorporated site realities into our designs from the outset.
We considered elements like cross walls, beams, switchboards, and fire safety features, ensuring our plans seamlessly integrated with the physical space.

## Minimizing Rework and Enhancing Precision

By starting with site measurements and considering site realities, we have virtually eliminated the need for reworks in our projects.

We make your designs effortlessly align with the actual space, resulting in a perfect fit for switchboards, harmonious integration of obstacles, and enhanced precision throughout.

Starting with accurate site measurements and considering site realities from the beginning of the design process has been a game-changer for us at Nestup Space. By understanding the space at its core, we have minimized rework and achieved a remarkable level of precision in our designs. This change has saved us time and resources while ensuring client satisfaction.

As interior design professionals, let's recognize the power of site measurements and the importance of incorporating site realities into our designs. By embracing this approach, we can ensure greater accuracy, reduce rework, and elevate the overall quality of our work.

I would love to hear your thoughts on this topic and any experiences you've had in dealing with site measurements and site realities during interior design projects. Let's continue to refine our processes and deliver exceptional results.
